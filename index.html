Work smart: start the machines first

In the first 10 minutes, get the machines working. They do the hard work while you clean.

  1. Start the robot. Empty its dirty water, fill the clean water, pick up cables and clothes from the floor, then press Start. It vacuums and mops everywhere. Never mop by hand.
  2. Load the dishwasher. Empty it, then load the big items: grill grates, plancha plate, grease tray, air fryer drawers and plates, coffee drip tray.
  3. Start the laundry 2 hours before you finish. Wash and dry takes up to 2 hours, and it must be folded before you leave.
  4. Work top to bottom. Dust high surfaces first. Floors last.
  5. Split the work. One person: kitchen and bathrooms. The other: bedroom, living room and balcony.
