The machines do the hard work while you clean.

  3. Laundry 2 hours before you finish, so it is dry and folded before you leave.
  4. Work top to bottom. Dust high surfaces first, floors last.
  5. Two people? One does the kitchen and bathrooms, the other the bedroom, living room and balcony.
